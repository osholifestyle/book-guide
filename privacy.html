Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

The Book Guide ("we", "the site") helps you understand non-fiction books before you buy them. This page explains, in plain words, what information the site uses and why.

In short: your account details stay in your own browser. We do not run ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not use analytics trackers at this time.

What you give us

When you create an account, you enter your email address and first name. You may also choose an age range, a country, how often you read, and your favourite topics. The site uses this only to pick your daily book and to greet you.

Where it is stored

Your account, saved books, reading progress and settings are stored in your browser on your own device (in "local storage"). They are not sent to a server run by us. This also means they do not follow you to another phone or browser, and clearing your browser data removes them. If we add online accounts in the future, we will update this page first and ask for your agreement.

Other services the site uses

  • Open Library and Google Books provide book covers and details. When a page loads these, their servers can see your IP address and the book being looked up. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • AI mini books. For books outside our library, the site sends the book title and author (not your account details) to an AI service through a Cloudflare worker, which uses Google's Gemini model to write the mini book.
  • Amazon. Buy buttons open Amazon in a new page. Amazon then applies its own privacy policy. See our Affiliate Disclosure.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs such as IP addresses.

Feedback

If you send feedback, we receive the message you write and, if you include it, your email address. We use it only to improve the site and, if you ask, to reply.

Your choices

  • Open Profile and choose Delete my account to erase your account, library and saved data from your browser.
  • You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings at any time.
  • You can use the site without giving real details, except that sign-up asks for an email address.

Children

The Book Guide is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you are under 18, please use the site with a parent or guardian's knowledge.

Changes

If we change how the site uses information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Use the Feedback button on the site to reach us with any question about your information.
